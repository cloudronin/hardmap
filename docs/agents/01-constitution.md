These are judgment laws. They are **authored**, not compiled, and they change only by owner ruling —
which is why they live in a file a person writes rather than in a table a build regenerates.

Each was paid for. Where a law has a scar, the scar is named, because a rule whose cost is invisible gets
optimised away by the next person who finds it inconvenient.

### Chat is not an artifact

A decision that exists only in a conversation does not exist. Rulings go into the maptrail, reasoning
into the methods thread, sequence into commits. If you find yourself about to rely on something "we
agreed earlier", stop and go read the artifact — and if the artifact does not say it, the agreement is
not binding, however clearly you remember it.

The corollary is stronger than the rule: **provenance is emitted at event time, by the machinery
performing the act.** A trail composed afterwards from what someone remembers is a reconstruction wearing
a record's clothes. This is why writing and recording are one step and not two.

### Prefer physics to guards

A guard is a rule someone must respect. Physics is a state the system cannot occupy. Where both are
available at comparable cost, take physics.

The frontier is the standing example: reserved rows are not withheld from capture, they are **never
captured at all**, so predictions are hashed before their frames exist. That is strictly stronger than
withholding frames that already exist, because it removes the person from the loop entirely. The write
surface being a separate binary is the same move: a verb absent from what ships cannot be typed by
someone who should not hold it.

**The scar:** three guards once checked row *names* and a reserved region was reproduced under a
different name. Every guard passed. Only an unrelated failure stopped the capture. Reservation is a
property of the region, not of the label above it.

### Expression before conclusion

Say what the machinery actually does before saying what it means. Investigate before proposing:
check whether the eight copies had really drifted rather than asserting the smell.

This is not thoroughness for its own sake. A conclusion reached before the expression is a hypothesis
that will recruit evidence; a conclusion reached after it is a finding. The difference shows up later, as
the thing nobody can reproduce.

### Measurement over declaration

Where a declaration and a measurement disagree, the measurement wins and the disagreement is
**preserved**, not resolved quietly.

A derived census once corrected a hand-written list of confounded rows — finding six where three had been
named, acquitting one that had been accused, and catching one inside an already-frozen artifact. Rows
that declare themselves flat but move are carried as `declared_flat_but_moves` rather than reconciled to
either side. The disagreement is the finding.

### Stop clean rather than ship tired

An unfinished thing in a known state beats a finished thing in an unknown one. Leave the tree green, the
artifacts fresh, the trail written, and the next question named. If you cannot finish, finish the part
that verifies and say plainly what you left.

### The front-door rule

**Act through `foundry` verbs. Never write an artifact directly.**

The verbs carry the emission, the refusals, and the guards. A hand-written artifact is a write that
escaped its own provenance: no maptrail record, no freshness stamp, no roster check, no reservation
guard. It will look exactly like a legitimate one and will be indistinguishable from it later — which is
the whole problem, because the archive's value is that every row in it can say where it came from.

If a verb does not exist for what you need, that is the finding. Say so and stop; do not route around
the front door by writing the file yourself. Adding the verb is cheap. Discovering in six months that one
artifact has no provenance and nobody can say which is not.

The same rule covers deletion and repair. Frozen bytes stay byte-identical forever, pre-reading
declarations are epistemically frozen even where they are not mechanically frozen, and append-only logs
are corrected by appending — never by editing the record that was wrong.
